For the broader profile with operator God chat, live-generated scalar systems,

## Try it

```sh
ARGOS_SPATIAL_SECONDS=300 npm run demo:spatial -- --live --lovecraft
```

Open the private loopback URL printed by the server, then press **Start world**.
The ready preview makes no model requests. Start enables ordinary paid NPC and
spirit cognition; the run is bounded to five minutes of wall or simulation time.
Stop world saves the run earlier. Without `--live`, this is a manual physics/interaction fixture,
not autonomous behavior. The command uses the existing models and assets.

`--lovecraft` selects the authored town, evolution and coastal-mystery story
profile together. Do not combine it with a substitute world, growth fixture,
household or Blackout scenario. The existing town/story commands remain unchanged.

## What this slice changes

- Same ten residents, room/prop identities, movement grid, collision and action
  anchors. Houses and public places receive a coherent Saltmere setting.
- Distinct private histories, attachments, disagreements and initial intentions.
  People can inspect real objects and compare accounts; recollections are not
  proof of current world state or knowledge available to every resident.
- Authored visible details on existing props. These are inspectable descriptions,
  not an implemented clue graph, inventory puzzle chain or predetermined answer.
- A shared world specification supplies the public coastal-mystery brief and
  private character creation seeds to their existing owners.
- Native simulation clock starts at 18:00 and fresh outdoor weather rows start
  cloudy. Opening weather spends no intervention budget and does not overwrite
  existing weather state. The renderer adds a dusk palette and coastal atmosphere
  without adding traversable locations or inventing simulation events.

The operator can select residents, follow them, read articulated thoughts, text
their phones and plant optional thoughts. Phone access still requires native
actions. Roof and camera controls remain presentation-only. The natural-language
Blackout house-spirit mutation box is **not** a town-wide God builder.

## Existing authority, not a new story engine

The Watcher observes public activity. The Arbiter checks consistency. The
Narrator receives an active, prospective coastal-horror brief and the existing
native weather and branch-event grants. The Weaver reviews evidenced needs;
God reviews catalog furnishing proposals. Residents independently choose their
responses. Requests and interpretations are displayed separately from effects
that actually committed.

The existing bounds remain: three weather changes at least sixty simulated
seconds apart; two branch incidents at least ninety seconds apart; four approved
furnishing sites/attempts; ten Weaver design reviews. No arbitrary object spawn,
monsters, hidden sounds, supernatural mind control or forced NPC reactions.
No coding-daemon jobs, new mechanics, model changes, H3, VR or asset downloads
are enabled by this profile. Art direction is not evidence a mystery event
occurred. Decorative water outside the map is not an accessible location.

## What to look for

Can residents pursue different concerns, inspect a concrete detail, compare
accounts and revise what they intend to do? Does a message change someone's
choices without becoming a command? Does the Narrator introduce a grounded
complication and observe its consequences rather than merely claiming drama?

Those are observation questions, not guaranteed outcomes. Native inspection can
deliver a description into private perception, but does not yet guarantee durable,
structured clue discovery. Prior failed household/live evidence remains failed;
this new setting does not close the ten-trial or ten-minute endurance gates.
