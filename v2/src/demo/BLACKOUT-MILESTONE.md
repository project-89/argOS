## Stage 1 — fixed-world physical repair

Implement and validate a small blackout puzzle with a complete authored world.
All required resources exist for this stage. Growth is disabled so an NPC's
investigation and execution can be assessed without a builder changing the task.

### Native contract

1. Fault, power state, repair prerequisites and any necessary part/tool state are
   explicit ECS data. Register their schemas before authoring instances.
2. Observations reveal only the information available at that location through
   an installed inspection/perception operation. An action menu or private
   narrator must not reveal the solution or a remote object's state.
3. Repair, inspection and item handling use the existing timed executor. Walking
   into reach, possession, safe preconditions and target identity are checked
   again at completion. Pending work changes no repair state.
4. Wrong parts, an unsafe sequence, changed/removed targets, interruption and
   source stop produce explicit failed/cancelled evidence without partial success
   claims. A failed attempt does not reset or silently fix the puzzle.
5. Only committed world effects establish restoration. Ordinary speech, an
   intention, a thought, a progress percentage or a scene cue cannot establish it.
6. Native outcomes return to the NPC's existing private working memory,
   experience/appraisal and continued activity. Public cues describe the physical
   action without publishing private interpretations or anticipated effects.

### Offline gates before a live check

- A deterministic operator-driven positive path proves the repair mechanics.
  Label this physics proof, not an autonomous solve.
- Negative paths cover incorrect order/part, missing possession, insufficient
  proximity, removed/recycled targets, mid-action changes, cancellation and source
  stop. There must be no early or duplicate effect.
- Solve-state predicates inspect native components and exact consumed/installed
  identities. Repeating a request cannot duplicate stock or a terminal effect.
- Scene and server regressions retain private-state separation, no pre-spoken
  dialogue, no success cues for failed/pending work, and simulation-time expiry.
- Existing spatial movement, seated departure, inventory, phone and construction
  tests still pass; typecheck and client presentation checks remain green.
- No hidden deterministic controller is moving characters during manual tests.
  No test or observation callback selects an action during an autonomy test.

### One prospective bounded live check, only after the offline gate

One approximately **180-second simulation** trial is the proposed first live
check, not an automatic sequence of retries. Before launching, record a protocol
with an independent hard wall deadline, HTTP-attempt/cost bound, exact starting
world, source/code fingerprints, unchanged model configuration, allowed stimuli
and stop/drain procedure. Root must finalize those bounds rather than infer them
from this note. A 180-second result does not satisfy endurance.

Observe rather than steer: no forced action, mid-run hint escalation, repair
mutation or injected success. Capture thoughts, selected purposes, native action
admissions/completions, perception/receipt timing, source identity, runtime
failures, request timing and pre-stop/final saves. Retain an incomplete solve as
evidence. Report the causal chain and bottleneck; do not relabel ordinary waiting
or banter as failure merely because it is less cinematic.

**Stage 1 acceptance:** sound native mechanics against the offline gates. If the
live check is launched, score its native repair gate prospectively as PASS/HOLD;
an honestly recorded failed attempt is evidence, not an acceptance pass. A
successful attempt does not by itself prove learning, repeatability or general
intelligence. Any blocker must be understood before expanding scope; do not
silently waive it or launch repeated attempts to obtain a better-looking result.

## Stage 2 — first exposure / retained experience / fresh comparison

Purpose: distinguish using remembered experience from memorizing a fixed answer,
following a stronger prompt, or simply receiving an easier world.

Predeclare a small comparison before model calls:

1. **First exposure:** the character encounters the fixed-world problem with its
   normal authored identity but no history of this puzzle. Preserve all outcomes,
   including mistaken actions and an incomplete attempt.
2. **Retained experience:** carry forward only that actor's actually acquired,
   private experiences through a validated memory import/restore boundary. Change
   the fault and relevant layout/resource placement. The old solution must not be
   sufficient without new observation.
3. **Fresh comparison:** use the same changed variant, persona, model/controller,
   runtime settings and opportunity/budget as the retained condition, but without
   the previous puzzle experiences. Fresh does not mean deprived of normal
   background identity or basic native capabilities.

The memory-transfer boundary is a gate, not an assumption. Full spatial resume is
not yet certified. Do not copy whole runtime component arrays, stale actions,
plans with execution authority, source capabilities, pending reservations or
foreign numerical entity references into the new episode. Preserve provenance
and actor privacy. Old receipts may be historical memories; they must never
count as native evidence that an action or repair happened in the new source.

Do not author a new success story, distill an evaluator's answer into a memory,
give only the retained condition a solution hint, or copy another NPC's private
knowledge. If a narrative summary is generated by an existing memory process,
retain its inputs/output and distinguish interpretation from committed facts.

Compare observation before action, appropriate question formation, adaptation to
the changed fault, unnecessary/repeated actions, stale assumptions, recovery,
time to first purposeful action and native repair outcome. Inspect which actual
memory was retrieved and how it influenced a decision. A solve is not proof that
the memory caused it; a failed solve may still demonstrate grounded recall.
This small comparison is exploratory, not a statistical substitute for the
ten-trial baseline.

**Stage 2 acceptance:** validated private-history continuity, no inherited
execution authority, matched conditions and a traceable qualitative comparison.
Publish both retained and fresh outcomes, not just the better one.

### The actual missing adapter

SpatialDemo currently exposes operator construction, not this autonomous path.
Simply enabling spirits can create native semantic rooms that the spatial
renderer/executor has not registered. A post-hoc renderer watcher is insufficient:
it can discover an already-committed but unusable room after allocation.

Add one narrow world/source-scoped adapter at the native place materialization
boundary:

1. **Prepare before any mutation:** map the exact approved native manifest to one
   reserved spatial shop footprint and supported catalog assets. Validate all
   geometry, doors, type mappings, reachable interaction anchors, existing room
   identities and the complete physical admission budget, including walls.
2. **Commit through native construction:** preserve the approved manifest and
   every source/demand/identity guard. Deterministic geometry must not add
   unapproved stock, people, mechanics or an evaluator-selected solution.
3. **Register before route publication:** bind allocated native identities to
   spatial floors, collision geometry, anchors and read-only visual assets before
   the materializer publishes connectivity/constructed readiness.
4. **Finish honestly:** construction and availability remain distinct from use.
   Preserve partial failures; never refund/retry them invisibly. A failed or
   unsupported manifest cannot report a useful shop.

Initial authored rooms also need native `enableRoomRouteTopology` and
`connectRoomRoute` connectivity consistent with the physical door policy. Current
geometric movement alone is not the route evidence used by architect admission.
Do not create two independently authoritative door/topology states.

### Authority, bounds and staleness

- Restrict Weaver before startup to entity proposals, one per cycle, God approval
  required and no direct execution. Keep coordinator `autoApproveProposals:false`
  and `maxProposalExecutions:1`. Disable other builders/coding for this proof.
- Reserve one **lifetime** shop slot with a declared finite stock/entity budget.
  One proposal per cycle is not a lifetime limit. Repeated observations, a failed
  build or consumed stock must not silently reset the slot.
- Preserve exact proposal specification and owner/source guards. A copied or
  edited proposal cannot inherit approval. Unsupported designs fail explicitly.
- Prefer a genuinely ongoing owned goal/undertaking as the demand citation;
  transient selected-purpose and failure-only observations can become stale as
  the actor changes activity or leaves. Do not prolong stale evidence to rescue a
  desired outcome.
- Coordinator cadence values are **ticks**, not milliseconds. The old fixture's
  30 ticks at 2 Hz is approximately 15 seconds; copying it unchanged into the 10 Hz
  spatial host would schedule approximately every 3 seconds.
- Keep the one-world-per-process host boundary; global static ECS stores are not
  proof of safe overlapping numeric IDs in several concurrent local worlds.

### Preserve puzzle consequences and prove usefulness

The shop may contain newly approved finite stock. It must not recreate the same
removed/consumed identity, repair the fault directly, put the part in an NPC's
inventory, teleport the NPC, force a choice or mark its goal complete. Repeated
requests after stock consumption must not replenish it automatically.

Required chain:

`actual unmet repair purpose → grounded Weaver proposal → native God approval →
validated spatial/native construction → independent NPC travel and acquisition →
real repair effect → receipt-bound feedback to Weaver/God`

Observe exact source/actor/proposal/place/part identities. Score physical
possession and repair components, not a description that a shop has useful parts.
Feedback must cite the post-use native receipt and complete while its actual
scope is current. A later unrelated successful review or plausible God prose
does not substitute for that feedback gate.
