## Evidence-based diagnosis

1. **The story cast is already richer than the plain town.**
   `spatial-town-story.ts` authors four formative memories per person, drives,
   named relationships and competing recollections. The plain town instead has
   one warm memory and mostly compatible eat/rest/walk purposes. Both enable
   the existing cognitive faculties when live. Increasing biography length is
   not a substitute for proving that experience changes decisions.
2. **Authored opening purposes bypass the newer intention lifecycle.**
   `spatial-demo.ts` seeds them through legacy `createIntentGoal`, without
   `GoalLifecycle`; `goal-pursuit.ts` cannot offer that intention for personal
   deferral/release. An active opening goal also blocks ordinary new-goal
   generation. In the saved four-minute story trial all ten opening goals lack
   the lifecycle, six remain active and four are expired; none completed or
   retired. This is a concrete integration gap, not proof it explains every
   flat decision.
3. **Useful spirit observation is lossy.**
   Weaver reviews roughly every 60 simulation seconds, but its fresh-event
   list contains only the most recent 30 seconds / 50 raw receipts. In
   `recordings/town-growth-check-74N0IN`, actual God approval and construction
   produced a chair Ivo independently used. The subsequent Weaver review
   missed that use; Tess's original seating/use gate remained false.
4. **State and setting prose can contradict one another.**
   The cafe-seat-need fixture permanently described no chairs and four pastries.
   Native construction/consumption does not rewrite that static description.
   A historical opening observation is legitimate; repeating it as the current
   room description is not.
5. **Existing narrative pressure is often irrelevant to available action.**
   `recordings/town-story-check-na3Hta` demonstrates a Narrator branch incident
   and subsequent review of its native receipt. Residents perceived damage but
   did not clear it. It did not obstruct their route or gathering. Ignoring it
   is not necessarily a cognition failure. Meanwhile the gathering encouraged
   promises about cups/napkins not installed in the physical world.
6. **A fast world clock does not prove fast cognition.**
   That four-minute run measured about 0.96x realtime, yet foreground queue delay
   reached 10.476s and background planning delay 36.143s. Keep queue, inference,
   admission and physical-execution latency separate.

- Three or four residents with different practical purposes, a named tie, a
  meaningful past experience and a competing concern each. Ordinary needs
  matter, but residents are not merely hunger/refill loops.
  A possible starting cast: Tess wants to host without doing all the work;
  Ivo is hungry but remembers promising to leave a pastry for someone else;
  Noor needs a seated break but does not want to disappoint Tess; Mara wants
  company while keeping a private relationship concern to herself. These are
  proposed circumstances, not required actions or newly implemented content.
- Purposes must connect to installed actions: an actual finite snack, usable
  seat, private phone, reachable place or inspectable/openable container. Do
  not imply cooking, reading arbitrary documents or fetching absent props.
- Observe an initial purpose through action to a verifiable outcome; then
  observe continuation, reconsideration or a new purpose. More thought text
  or repeated promises do not establish progress.
- Preserve separate private views: knowing, hearing, seeing and remembering
  are not interchangeable. A witness can tell someone; a narrator cannot make
  its overview everyone's knowledge.
- Use a complication that can change an evidenced purpose's available choices.
  An isolated operator-induced condition tests perception/adaptation only.
  A separate autonomous spirit choice tests direction. Never describe one as
  the other or require a specific emotion/cleanup response to manufacture a pass.
- Require the actual committed result, relevant owned memory, and a subsequent
  accepted spirit review containing its exact receipt IDs. A proposed event,
  attractive prose, approval or UI entry is not execution or feedback.
- Include a later changed condition to test whether retained experience is
  used appropriately. Compare with a separately initialized control; do not
  erase memory mid-run or call a repeated scripted solution learned behavior.
  Review the evidence available **before** a choice; an after-the-fact line
  saying "I remember" is not by itself proof that memory caused that choice.
