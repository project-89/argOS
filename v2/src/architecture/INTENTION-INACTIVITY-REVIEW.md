## Ownership and boundaries

- Goal/GoalLifecycle retain the actor's intention. OngoingActivity remains the
  chosen organizing activity; an admitted background goal cannot replace it.
- Inactivity of a self-directed subjective episodic intention requests review.
  The existing Goal becomes paused. GoalLifecycle.reviewPendingJson binds that
  historical request to the owner and exact goal episode. Its timer is simulation
  time, not elapsed inference, renderer time or wall time spent offline.
- Old plans retire, preserving definitions and cursors. Ongoing activity grants
  are invalidated, not rewritten as a new actor choice. A matching immediate
  continuation needs revision. Native in-flight effects still produce receipts.
- GoalLifecycle.reviewRevision fences active -> paused -> active transitions.
  Old foreground actions, background plans, personal reviews, intention revisions
  and native goal-credit bindings cannot regain validity merely because status
  returned to active. It is an execution fence, not another progress ledger.
- The existing foreground goalReview can retain, defer or release a pending
  intention. Retain preserves its identity and grants a new reconsideration
  window. It grants no same-turn pursuit or old action/plan execution. A fresh
  later action is checked normally. Defer uses the existing actor-selected wake
  and timer; release is an explicit actor decision, never success proof.
- Ignoring review preserves the paused intention. It does not automatically
  resume or create further review calls each tick. Pending self-directed goals
  count toward the same bounded capacity (five unsettled subjective intentions,
  including standing and paused). New duplicates of active/paused goals are
  rejected. No eviction is described as an actor's abandonment.

## Expiry and compatibility

Explicit per-goal inactivity budgets remain expiry policy. Verified/authored
objectives and legacy non-lifecycle goals keep their existing semantics. New
subjective intentions no longer receive an invented ten-minute deadline. Saved
deadlines are not guessed away or migrated; explicit deadlines remain terminal
even while an intention is pending review. Reconsideration cannot extend one.
The existing initial source-anchored deferral behavior is otherwise unchanged.

Cleanup retains valid pending review, including exploratory intentions, instead
of collecting it as a settled outcome. Parent-goal invalidation remains effective.
Save/reload retains historical review evidence, not an in-process draft or action
permit. A new review needs fresh owner/source/persona and goal-definition checks.
