interactive small-world demonstration: actors sustain or deliberately revise

memory survive appropriately, reusable ECS mechanics remain authoritative, and

human-level behavior, unlimited autonomous genesis, an MMO, or a rewrite.

## Invariants

- ECS components and relations own physical truth. Intent, action admission,
  progress, completion, subjective interpretation and rendered depiction are
  distinct. A sentence about success does not execute or prove success.
- NPCs own choices. No compulsory replies, scripted grief, emotional closure
  targets, forced departures, hard topic quotas or lexical thought filtering.
- Perception stays responsive. Quiet/listening are legitimate activities;
  reduced model traffic must not make actors deaf or postpone chosen deadlines.
- Separate fast foreground control from slower planning, appraisal, reflection,
  narration and engineering. Stale background results cannot acquire authority.
- Preserve actual evidence and its scope: unread message bodies remain private;
  another person's thoughts are not public facts; heard assertions are attributed.
- Persist meaningful intent and knowledge, not executable callbacks, active
  leases, canceled commands, host permissions or pending model authority.
- Preserve existing user edits, recordings, secrets and the running demo. No
  unrelated cleanup, destructive reset, unsolicited commit, or server restart.
- No H3 calls until simulation gates pass and a bounded provider test's session
  duration/spend are agreed. No unattended recurring automation is requested.
