## Evidence ownership

| Record | Projected information | Dependency lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| goal | Existing owned active intention, priority | Same goal episode/status/definition/review lifetime |
| undertaking | Actor's interpreted ongoing task, optional concrete target | Same owned work episode; survives supporting-goal retirement and ordinary progress/travel; release, replacement or completion invalidates it |
| planned_step | Upcoming physical action type and exact target, linked goal | Same native plan/step episode and goal; earlier steps may advance, but the cited step must remain ahead/current and unchanged |
| selected_purpose | Selected purpose plus physical action/target, including a genuinely blocked approach | Current actor-controlled purpose/target; no restored or externally edited command authority |

All records use the existing opaque intention references and source-scoped binders. They are author observations, not execution permissions or public events. Once a bound observation is found stale, it cannot revive. Only original capture objects can resolve references to real actors in construction contracts.

The view is bounded: 64 records overall; up to eight goals, three upcoming targets and three ongoing undertakings per actor, plus its selected purpose. Omission counts remain explicit. No raw speech/message contents, plan command arguments, private proofs or autobiographical memories are copied into the new fields. New residents must not inherit another actor's private knowledge.

## Need interpretation and safety

Actor demand precedes machinery in the prompt. Weaver compares it with actual entities, room routes, available capabilities, existing services, pending builds and construction budget. Error-free systems do not imply a sufficiently developed world. Plans or commitments can support proposing a destination, resource or service before execution fails. A mere passing remark, ambiguous reference, intentional obstacle or adequately supported purpose may still warrant no construction.

Current object states and removed identities are visible in author context. Consumed/removed objects are not empty slots to refill; truncated catalogs are not proof of absence. Designs cannot recreate observed or newly removed identities under the same name, including room contents. This exact-name guard is not a semantic guarantee against every conceivable puzzle-breaking alternative; the model must still make grounded judgments under existing approval and support contracts.

Primary proposal types respect the configured entity/system/component permissions. Existing God approval, bounded construction, route/source/actor checks and native support validation remain. Cited ongoing undertakings, like active goals, may retain demand while the actor travels. Construction neither moves an actor nor completes its goal or promise.
